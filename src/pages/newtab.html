Welcome to HEoBS

HEoBS already blocks ads, trackers, pop-ups and known dangerous sites. Nothing needs setting up for that.

One optional extra needs you: Google Safe Browsing adds Google's much larger list of dangerous sites. Google only gives the key for it to you personally, so HEoBS cannot set it up on your behalf. It is free and takes about two minutes.
